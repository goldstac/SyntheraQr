## ✨ Overview

**SyntheraQR** is a next-generation **QR code generator website** built to combine design and functionality.  
It transforms your links, text, or data into **beautiful, customizable QR codes** with just a few clicks —  
from clean minimal looks to bold gradient designs.

Think of it as **“the modern way to generate QR codes”** —  
sleek, interactive, and built for creators, developers, and brands who care about presentation.

## 🚀 Features

- 🎨 **Customizable Designs:** Choose colors of the background and foreground.  
- ⚙️ **Smart Generation:** Detects URLs, text, and special formats automatically.  
- 📷 **High-Resolution Output:** Export crisp PNG, SVG, JPG or WEBP QR codes for printing or digital use.  
- ⚡ **Instant Preview:** See your QR update in real time as you type.  
- 🌈 **Clean UI:** Simple, elegant, and responsive across all devices.  

## 🧩 How It Works

1. Enter your text, link, or data in the input field.  
2. Customize your QR code with colors.  
3. Instantly preview the result in real time.  
4. Export your final QR as your preferred filetype — ready for print or digital sharing.
